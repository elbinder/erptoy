# erptoy

**Making good business fun again.**

erptoy is a small, well-made toolkit for running a real business: orders, stock, people, pay and the books. Think of it like a box of good bricks rather than a cheap plastic toy. Simple pieces, built to last, that fit together the way you need them to.

## Why

Most business software is built to extract value: more fees, more lock-in, more reports nobody reads. erptoy is built to create value for everyone the business touches. Customers, owners, suppliers, and most of all the people on the lowest pay, whose week depends on the schedule being right and the pay arriving on time.

"Good" here means good for all stakeholders, not just shareholders.

## Where it comes from

erptoy grows out of running Ronny's Pizza in Tbilisi, Georgia since 2009: five stores, a production kitchen, and years of wrestling with software that made simple things hard. The pieces are proven on a real floor before they come here.
